# ADR-122 — Final Standalone Play-Consistency Research Screen

Step 87B passed historical validation.

Step 87C is the final planned standalone feature-family screen before Project
Gridiron pivots away from feature discovery.

Four isolated signals are tested:

1. offensive success-rate advantage;
2. defensive success-prevention advantage;
3. combined success-rate matchup advantage;
4. negative-play matchup advantage.

Each signal is screened at weights 0.25, 0.50, 1.00, and 2.00 against the
locked six-weight baseline. All previously rejected research families remain
at zero.

Step 87D will either narrow a credible winner for robustness or reject and park
the family. After Step 87, development pivots to combined-model validation,
calibration, and market/betting integration.
